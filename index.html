Super-exponential growth in the agentic era

The AI economy is small at the moment, and world GDP is dominated by human production. But the AI economy is growing larger every day. Once agents become autonomous and spawn other agents, and those agents create other agents, and machines build machines, there is no limit to the agentic economy. It is going to explode.
